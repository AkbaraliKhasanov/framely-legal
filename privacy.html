Privacy Policy

Framely - Last updated: September 2026

Framely does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Everything the app does - browsing your videos, extracting frames, adjusting export settings - happens entirely on your device.

Photo & Video Library

Framely asks for access to your Photos library so you can browse your videos and Live Photos and pick frames to extract. When you export a frame, Framely also asks to add the resulting image back to your library. Your videos and photos are never uploaded anywhere - Framely reads and writes to your library locally and only when you're actively using the app.

Camera & Microphone

If you choose to record a new video from within Framely, the app requests camera and microphone access for that recording only. Recorded video stays on your device like any other video in your library, until you choose to export or share it yourself.

Location & Frame Metadata

Some videos carry embedded GPS location and camera information. Framely can optionally include this metadata in the frames you export - this is a setting you control in Settings. Any location or camera data written into an exported image stays in that file on your device; Framely never reads or transmits it anywhere on its own.

Purchases

Framely Pro subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Framely never sees or stores your payment details - only a cryptographically signed confirmation from Apple that a purchase is active, which the app uses to unlock Pro features.

Analytics

Framely does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK. There is no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind.

Framely includes an optional, off-by-default analytics toggle in Settings ("Share Analytics"). When turned on, Framely records a small number of anonymous usage events - the Pro paywall being shown, a purchase starting, completing, or failing, and a frame export completing or failing (including the export format and number of frames). These events never include your videos, photos, exported images, filenames, file paths, or any other content.

In this version those events stay entirely on your device (written to the local system log and a small local counter) and are never transmitted anywhere. If a future version adds a real analytics backend, this policy will be updated first to say so explicitly, and the same off-by-default toggle will still gate it.

Crash & Performance Diagnostics

Framely uses Apple's built-in MetricKit framework to receive crash, hang, and performance diagnostics from iOS. This is a standard, Apple-operated system capability that works independently of the analytics toggle above. Framely's own use of it is limited to writing diagnostic summaries to the local system log for debugging - never transmitted anywhere by the app itself.

Children's Privacy

Framely is not directed at children under 13. Since the app doesn't collect personal data from any user regardless of age, no additional children's-privacy handling is needed beyond what's described above.

Changes to This Policy

If Framely's data practices ever change, this page will be updated first and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
