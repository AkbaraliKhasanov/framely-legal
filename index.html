Common questions

How do I cancel my Framely Pro subscription?
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Framely Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the Framely Pro screen and tap Restore in the top-right corner, using the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything happens on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
